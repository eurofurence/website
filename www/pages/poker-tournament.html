EF30 Charity Poker Tournament Rules & Regulations

A short preamble from your Host, Ace!

This event is a bonus activity for charity and does not award any payouts.
It is purely of a recreational nature. 1st, 2nd and 3rd place will receive honorary recognition as "Eurofurence Charity Poker Champion 2026", as well as a small token for the memories with no market value!

You can claim a spot in the tournament after donating €15 to charity in the Charity Booth. Remember to state that this is your intent, then follow the instructions given in a timely manner.

There are 20 slots in the tournament - 2 preliminary tables will be played on (day 1). The top 5 players from each table progress to the finals on (day 2). Participation vouchers will be given out. Registration is first come first serve. If all slots fill, we will keep a waiting list of 10 players to fall back on.

If you are a professional player or regularly play to make money online, please consider abstaining from playing.

New players are expected to know the rules of Texas 'Holdem. Questions can be answered during games, but will be kept concise to not stretch our timeslot. Should you feel the need to ask more questions, or talk and reflect on the game after the tournament, the hosts will happily be available after the game is over.

A few notes for etiquette:

  1. Please be respectful to both hosts and other players!
    Rude behavior and/or disruption of the game will not be tolerated. Repeated offence can lead to expulsion. The host has the final say in matters.
  2. Please announce your actions verbally (i.e. - "Call", "Fold", "raise to 50", "reraise 100") if possible
    and do not act out of turn. Acting out of turn always stands, unless raising out turn when there is an additional raise by some other player. Mucking cards on showdown (first to show after river) is forbidden. Cards are flipped only when there is no more action.
  3. If you leave the table, you will be auto-folding on your action. Blinds will be collected accordingly to your
    position. Please try your best to not leave the table.
  4. We reserve the rights to on-the-spot increase blinds, should the event face time constraints and need to make space. This will not occur until 2 players are left (heads-up)
  5. Banter and playful conversation is allowed and endorsed within the limits of rule 1, but don't do angle shoots (i.e. attempting to manipulate other players either verbally or by mime or gestures). Don't talk, hint or gesture at what your hand's value is when you're not in the hand either.
  6. Last but very much not least: Remember to enjoy the game! If you tilt, try your best to keep up the spirit and excitement for the remaining players.

See you at the poker tables!
Ace
